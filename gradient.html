ABOUT ME

I'm a Computer Science student, developer, and creative thinker who believes that coding is more than just writing lines of code — it's a way of bringing ideas to life. I code with creativity, build webpages with heart, and love turning simple ideas into experiences that feel unique and meaningful.

I'm currently exploring the world of Java, web development, and UI/UX, constantly learning new technologies and finding better ways to create. I'm still growing, still experimenting, and definitely still figuring things out — but that's what makes the journey exciting.

I don't just want to write code that works. I want to create things that people enjoy experiencing.
